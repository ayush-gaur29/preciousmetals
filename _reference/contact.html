Frequently Asked Questions

How does Precious Metals Consulting facilitate diamond mining investments in Angola?

We provide end-to-end guidance, from securing exploration rights and permits through ENDIAMA and the Ministry of Mineral Resources to on-the-ground geological surveys, operational equipment deployment, and Kimberly Process certification for international sales.

What regulatory compliance is guaranteed for international investors?

All transactions and joint ventures are structured in complete compliance with Angolan private investment laws (AIPEX), international anti-money laundering frameworks, and Kimberley Process diamond origin certifications.

Can your team assist with real estate acquisitions and construction in Luanda?

Yes. We manage prime land title searches, architectural feasibility, contractor vetting, and municipal construction approvals for commercial, hospitality, and residential properties.
